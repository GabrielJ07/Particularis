01 / The Human Story

Some connections never leave us.

There are places where history is not simply preserved. It is felt. In Old Town San Diego, Día de los Muertos brings remembrance into the present through marigolds, candles, photographs, music, and gatherings that honor those who came before us.

Every detail carries a story. Every individual carries a different memory. What we remember, we carry.

02 / A Particular Place

Old Town.
Many histories.
One encounter.

The architecture, streets, and cultural expressions of Old Town provide the setting for PARTICULARIS Cultural Study 001. We observe a place shaped by generations and interpret its visual details through original artwork.

The study does not claim to define or own the tradition. It invites closer observation, with respect for the Mexican communities whose practices give Día de los Muertos its enduring meaning.

05 / The Ecology of Intention

Created only when chosen.

PARTICULARIS does not manufacture speculative inventory in search of customers. Each physical artifact is made only after an individual chooses to acquire it.

No planned dead stock. No clearance cycle. No promotional discounting. A finite acquisition window, followed by the retirement of that edition.

Made-to-order production avoids unsold speculative stock. Materials, printing, and delivery still have environmental impacts.
